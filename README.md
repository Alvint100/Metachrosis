# Metachrosis
A game about an explorer fighting off enemies and bosses.

Originally was made to be a game with a more in depth story about the explorer fighting and destroying the person who sent people to attack him as well as many more mechanics such as gliding, swimming, combos, etc. but were cut due to time restraints as this was for a class project.

# Metachrosis

## Overview

A game about an explorer protecting his home from the forces of evil.

**Link to game:** https://tangerin4.itch.io/metachrosis

## About
The game is about the time when a town was invaded by the forces of evil and it was one person's goal to stop the attack. He fought and learned skills from the enemies he defeated along the way to beat the evil spirit of the forest.

## Tech Stack
- **Engine:** Unity
- **Language:** C#
- **Tools/Packages:** TextMeshPro, Mixamo, Input System, Unity Asset Store, Unity Version Control

## Features
- Custom combo system with light and heavy attacks
- Custom dynamic camera "rail" system (camera moves along a rail to help with vision)
- Enemy AI and player tracking
- 3 different levels, each with unique designs and layouts to challenge player

## My Role - Team of 3
- Lead Game Designer and Level Designer
- Designed and tested different levels to align with difficulty curve
- Wrote a basic storyline and built levels that identify with the theme of the game
- Wrote scripts to help with player movement and switching between levels and menus

## Challenges & What I Learned
- Designing an interesting story is difficult. So often did I find myself going back to very generic plots instead of something fresh. To combat this, I would try to explore more into different games to see what made them unique and what I could do to implement something new into the game. So the idea of gaining powers came to mind, and then a story of an invasion (while still generic, it was changed to be a newer idea to keep it from being stale), and eventually, Metachrosis.

## Controls
Move - WASD
Jump - Space Bar
Light Attack - Left Click
Heavy Attack - Right Click
Dash - Shift
